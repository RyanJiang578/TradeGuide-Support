Frequently asked questions

Answers to common questions about TradeGuide.

What is TradeGuide?

TradeGuide is an educational app that uses AI to help users explore publicly traded stocks and review possible future price scenarios. It does not place trades or manage investment accounts.

How do I evaluate a stock?

Open the stock search screen, enter a name or ticker, select a suggestion, and submit your request. Your evaluation depends on the current availability of the app and its data services.

Why can't I submit another evaluation?

TradeGuide currently limits each account to 25 evaluations per day. If you've reached that limit, try again after the daily limit resets. You may also need to select a valid stock from the search suggestions.

Where can I find previous evaluations?

Recent evaluations appear on your Dashboard. TradeGuide currently shows evaluation history for up to seven days.

Are the stock predictions guaranteed?

No. AI-generated projections are speculative and can be inaccurate. They are for informational and educational purposes only and should not be treated as financial advice or a guarantee of future performance.

What should I do if I can't log in?

Check your email address and password, confirm your internet connection, and try reopening the app. If the issue continues, contact support using the information below. Never send your password.

How can I request account deletion or ask about my data?

Contact support using the email address below and state that you want to delete your account or have a privacy question. Support requests are separate from any account-deletion controls available within the app.
