Privacy Policy

How TradeGuide handles account information, AI analysis requests, and evaluation history.

Effective date: November 6, 2026

1. Overview

TradeGuide ("TradeGuide," "we," "us") is an educational stock analysis application operated by Ryan Jiang. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, shared, and retained when you use the TradeGuide mobile app and related services.

2. Information we collect

  • Account information: email address, password, and username. Passwords are processed and stored as password hashes rather than plain-text passwords.
  • Preferences: selected risk level and analysis timeframe.
  • Evaluation and usage information: stock symbols you evaluate; market prices, AI-generated projections and explanations; evaluation dates; and counts used to enforce the daily evaluation limit.
  • Authentication and operations: authentication tokens are used to secure requests. Our hosting infrastructure and service providers may process technical information such as request timestamps, IP addresses and diagnostic data necessary for operations and security.

TradeGuide does not connect to brokerage accounts, execute trades, or request payment-card information for its current free stock-evaluation features.

3. How we use information

  • Create, authenticate, maintain, and secure accounts.
  • Apply your analysis settings and generate requested stock evaluations.
  • Display recent evaluation history and enforce the daily limit of 25 evaluations per account.
  • Maintain service reliability, investigate errors or misuse, and answer support requests.
  • Comply with applicable legal requirements.

4. AI analysis and third-party services

When you request an evaluation, TradeGuide sends information needed to generate it to OpenRouter, which routes requests to an AI model provider. This may include the selected stock symbol or name, stock price, chosen risk level, and timeframe. The response is returned to TradeGuide and may be saved in your evaluation history. Please do not put sensitive personal information into stock search fields.

TradeGuide uses market-data services such as Alpha Vantage to retrieve stock prices, and Railway or related hosting infrastructure to operate the backend and database. These providers may process technical or request information required to supply their services. OpenRouter and downstream model providers may have their own processing, logging, retention, and training policies; their settings and applicable policies can differ. See OpenRouter provider privacy information.

5. Sale, advertising, and sharing

TradeGuide does not sell personal information or share it for targeted advertising. We share information with service providers when necessary to operate the app, such as processing AI requests, supplying market data, maintaining hosting, and handling legal obligations. We do not represent that an AI request is processed exclusively on TradeGuide's servers.

6. Storage and retention

TradeGuide displays evaluation history for the most recent seven days. Older evaluations are no longer returned in the app's history view. The seven-day display window does not by itself guarantee that all older database records, server backups, or provider records are erased at exactly seven days. Account and preference information is retained while the account remains active, and may be retained longer when necessary for security, legal, or operational reasons. Different third-party providers have their own retention schedules.

7. Your choices and deletion

You may update your username, risk level, and analysis timeframe in the app. To request deletion of your TradeGuide account and associated data, use the account-deletion option in the app if available, or contact ryanjiang578@gmail.com. We may need to verify your request. We will remove account-related information as applicable, subject to legal retention requirements and technical limitations such as backups or third-party retention.

To ask about your data or request correction, email the same address. Deleting the mobile app alone does not automatically delete your server-side account.

8. Security

TradeGuide uses measures including password hashing and token-based authentication to protect accounts. No online system can guarantee absolute security. Do not share your password or authentication tokens with anyone, including support.

9. Children's privacy

TradeGuide is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has submitted personal information, contact us so we can review and address the situation.

10. International access

Depending on where you are located and where service providers operate, information may be processed in other countries. Privacy rights and rules may vary by jurisdiction.

11. Updates to this policy

We may update this policy as TradeGuide changes or legal requirements evolve. We will post an updated version with a revised effective date, and provide additional notice when required.
